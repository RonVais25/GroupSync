תיאור התרחיש: יואב נכנס למערכת ורואה שהוקצו לו משימות חדשות. הוא פותח אחת מהן, קורא הערכת מאמץ של Scout, מאמת או מעדכן אותה, מתכנן את הזמן ביומן האישי עם הצעת חלוקה מ-Scout, ולבסוף מאשר שהמשימה נקלטה. הדגש הוא על אופטימיזציה של זרימת עבודה אישית — לא רק 'רואה משימה' אלא גם מתכנן ביצוע.

# GroupSync Wireframe

## App

Mobile app for university students managing group projects. An AI agent called Scout handles operations (reminders, summaries, effort estimates, scheduling). Scout proposes, never decides socially. Two roles:

- Leader (נועה): operations cockpit

- Member (יואב): personal inbox

Philosophy: calm, personal-first, NOT a chat app. Every Scout suggestion has an expandable "למה?" (why). Private actions auto execute, social actions need explicit approval.

## Output

One self-contained HTML file. Tailwind, Hebrew font Heebo, `dir="rtl"`. A storyboard: two rows of phone-frame mockups (leader on top, student below), reading right-to-left with labeled arrows between screens. At the bottom of the page, render a small summary table (one row per screen) listing which HTA steps each screen covers.

Each screen has a caption beneath it:

- Screen name (Hebrew) + HTA reference

- 3 to 5 feature bullets (what's on the screen)

- Transition trigger (what advances to the next screen)

## Style

Lo-fi but polished. Primary `{PRIMARY:cloud-blue}`, Scout accent `{SCOUT:light-purple}`, status red/yellow/green. White cards with a thin border, 12px radius. Lucide icons.

## Scout patterns (enforce on every Scout element)

Scout appears in three forms only:

1. Inline card: purple side-border, Scout label, suggestion, expandable "למה?", action row

2. Bottom sheet: for decisions, grab handle, options, sticky CTA

3. Notification banner: top of screen, light purple tint

Every Scout element must show: label / suggestion / "למה?" expandable / action row.

In screen L4, add numbered annotation callouts (1 to 4) on the right side of the phone frame pointing to the four parts of Scout's recommendation. This is the anatomy demo.
